# Comparative Analysis of Polar Coded F-OFDM and UFMC 5G NR Waveforms

## Overview

This project presents a comparative study of Polar-coded F-OFDM and Polar-coded UFMC waveforms for 5G New Radio (5G NR) wireless communication systems.

The study evaluates the performance of Polar-coded F-OFDM (PC-F-OFDM) and Polar-coded UFMC (PC-UFMC) systems under different wireless communication scenarios, including AWGN and Massive MIMO environments.

## Research Focus

The research investigates the performance of:

- Polar-coded F-OFDM (PC-F-OFDM)
- Polar-coded UFMC (PC-UFMC)
- 5G NR waveform techniques
- Massive MIMO systems
- Polar channel coding
- AWGN channel performance

## Performance Analysis

The comparative study considers communication-system performance using parameters such as:
- Bit Error Rate (BER)
- Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR)
- Waveform characteristics
- Polar coding performance
- AWGN channel
- Massive MIMO configuration

## Key Research Contribution

The study provides a comparative analysis of Polar-coded F-OFDM and Polar-coded UFMC systems for 5G NR communication.
The published study reports different relative performance characteristics for the two waveforms depending on the considered communication scenario, including AWGN and Massive MIMO environments.
