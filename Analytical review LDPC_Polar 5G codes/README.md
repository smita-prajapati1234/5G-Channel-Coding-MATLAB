# 5G NR Channel Coding – Conference Paper

## Overview

This project presents research work related to channel coding techniques for 5G New Radio (5G NR) wireless communication systems.

The work was presented as a conference paper and contributes to the study and analysis of channel coding 
approaches used for reliable communication in modern wireless networks.

## Research Focus

The paper focuses on channel coding aspects of modern wireless communication systems, 
with particular relevance to 5G NR communication.

The study contributes to understanding the role of channel coding in improving 
the reliability and performance of wireless communication systems.
